## Core Features

### Scam Detection

**What It Detects**:
- Phishing SMS messages
- Fake KYC update requests
- Suspicious links (bit.ly, shortened URLs)
- Predatory loan offers
- Data harvesting attempts

**How to Use**:
1. Tap "Check SMS" or paste message in chat
2. Agent analyzes for threats
3. See color-coded verdict card:
   - **RED**: Danger - do not proceed
   - **YELLOW**: Caution - be careful
   - **GREEN**: Safe - no threats detected

**Red Alert Screen**:
- If scam detected, screen flashes red
- Phone vibrates (pattern: buzz-buzz-buzz)
- Large warning: "SCAM DETECTED"
- Auto-dismisses after 3 seconds

### Loan Transparency Calculator

**Purpose**: Expose hidden costs in loan offers

**How to Use**:
1. Tap "Loan Calculator" button
2. Click "Take Screenshot or Upload Image"
3. Capture/upload loan app screenshot
4. Agent analyzes document

**What You'll See**:
- Stated Interest Rate (what they tell you)
- Effective Interest Rate (what you actually pay)
- Hidden charges list (processing fees, documentation charges)
- Progress bar showing cost difference
- RBI compliance status
- Recommended action

**Example**:
```
Loan Offer Says: 2% monthly interest
Reality: 28% annual + ₹700 in fees
Progress Bar: 75% hidden costs
Verdict: RED - Unsafe loan
```

### Government Benefits Discovery

**How to Use**:
1. Tap "Find Benefits"
2. Tell the agent:
   - Your occupation (farmer, worker, self-employed)
   - Your location (state/district)
   - Your income level
   - Land size (if farmer)

**What You'll Get**:
- List of matched schemes
- Eligibility percentage (90% match = high eligibility)
- Benefit amounts (₹6,000 per year)
- Priority level (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW)
- Required documents
- Next steps to apply

**Example Schemes**:
- PM-Kisan (farmers)
- Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (bank accounts)
- Subsidy programs
- State-specific benefits

## Emergency Features

### Panic Button (Red Circle - Bottom Right)

**When to Use**:
- Someone is pressuring you about a loan
- Aggressive loan shark calls
- Feeling threatened about money
- Need immediate help

**How It Works**:
1. Tap the red panic button
2. Records 10 seconds of audio automatically
3. Sends emergency report to agent
4. Agent provides immediate safety guidance
5. Suggests: "Block number, do not share OTP/Aadhaar"

**Safety Tips**:
- Never share OTP over phone
- Never share Aadhaar/PAN to unknown callers
- Do not click links in pressure messages
- Bank/government never calls asking for passwords

## Understanding Verdict Cards

### Red Card - DANGER
**Icon**: Alert triangle
**Meaning**: Serious threat detected
**Actions**:
- Block Sender button
- Report Scam button
- Do NOT proceed with offer/link
- Do NOT share any information

**Common Red Flags**:
- Urgent language ("Account will be blocked!")
- Threats ("Legal action if you don't pay")
- Requests for OTP/password
- Too-good-to-be-true offers
- Shortened/suspicious links

### Yellow Card - CAUTION
**Icon**: Warning triangle
**Meaning**: Suspicious patterns found
**Actions**:
- Verify with official sources
- Do not rush into decisions
- Ask questions
- Get second opinion

### Green Card - SAFE
**Icon**: Check circle
**Meaning**: No threats detected
**Note**: Still verify important decisions

### Blue Card - BENEFITS
**Icon**: Gift box
**Meaning**: Government scheme matched
**Actions**:
- Review eligibility
- Check required documents
- Start application process

## Understanding Loan Documents

### What Document Analyzer Checks

**Interest Rate Analysis**:
- Stated rate (what they advertise)
- Processing fees
- Documentation charges
- Late payment penalties
- True Effective Interest Rate (EIR)

**RBI Compliance**:
- Max 36% for NBFCs (legal limit)
- Proper disclosure requirements
- Mandatory loan agreement clauses

**Hidden Charges**:
- Setup fees
- Insurance bundled costs
- "Platform fees"
- Prepayment penalties

### Red Flags in Loan Documents

1. **Interest rate over 36% annually**
2. **Missing disclosures**
3. **Fees not clearly listed**
4. **Pressure to sign quickly**
5. **No cooling-off period**
6. **Unclear repayment terms**

## Safety Guidelines

### Never Share

- OTP (one-time password)
- Aadhaar number to unknown callers
- PAN card details
- Bank account password
- Credit/debit card CVV
- ATM PIN

### Verify Before Acting

- Government never asks for money to release benefits
- Banks never call asking for passwords
- Real loan officers meet in person
- Check official website domains (.gov.in)

### Red Flags

- Urgent language ("Act now or lose money!")
- Threats ("Police will arrest you")
- Too good to be true (0% interest)
- Upfront fees before loan approval
- No physical office address

## Tips for Best Results

### When Checking SMS

Paste the **entire message** including:
- Sender name/number
- Full text
- Any links
- Don't remove anything

### When Finding Benefits

Provide complete info:
- Exact occupation (small farmer, daily wage worker)
- State and district
- Annual income estimate
- Family size
- Land ownership (if applicable)

### When Analyzing Loans

Upload **clear photos** showing:
- Interest rate section
- Fees breakdown
- Repayment schedule
- Terms and conditions
- Company name

## Troubleshooting

### "File too large" error
- Max file size: 10MB
- Compress image before uploading
- Use screenshot instead of full photo

### Voice not working
- Allow microphone permission in browser
- Check device microphone settings
- Try typing instead

### No response from agent
- Check internet connection
- Refresh page
- Try simpler question
